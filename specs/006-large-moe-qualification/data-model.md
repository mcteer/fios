# Data model · Pair-only qualification

Extend the existing profile, job, receipts and catalog. Use additive tables/versions in the native ledger. Legacy manifests and active single-host profiles keep their original interpretation. The following are the new constrained fields. Other identities, grants, ownership, deadlines and receipt keys inherit the [paired contract](../005-paired-studio-inference/contracts/pair.md).

| Canonical field | Invariant |
| --- | --- |
| `Candidate.placement` | Immutable class `studio_pair_only` bound to the reviewed artifact digest across aliases. Exactly studio-a and studio-b in their assigned ranks, concurrency one. Validate again before native load. |
| `Candidate.limits` | 8,192 total tokens and at most 1,024 output, including template overhead. Enforce both caps. One 900-second absolute serving deadline covers queue, loading, generation and settlement. |
| `Preflight.basis` | References a passing hardware 005 qualification for this comparable path and current Studio identities. Records a stage-specific decision and reason bound to the candidate, pinned adapter, both Studios and their budgets. Probe approval lists only reviewed probe artifacts with digests and byte ceilings. Full-pack approval additionally references a successful cleaned two-rank probe for the same candidate and adapter, the exact pack digest and fresh full-size disk/memory checks per Studio. Preserve each decision and the calculated single-host disposition. No one-rank candidate job is admissible. |
| `Run.suite` | SHA-256 digest of the frozen workload, thresholds, rubric, settings, policy and fixture definitions. Bind each job to one case, repetition and suite before dispatch. |
| `Phase.observation` | One authenticated summary per rank for load, prefill and decode on success, tagged with attempt and phase. Require monotonic ordering, first/last sample, max sample gap, minimum headroom in bytes, maximum used swap relative to baseline, worst pressure, peak physical footprint. Missing intervals fail closed. |
| `Run.timing` | Nonnegative elapsed times with monotonic ordering, generated and input token counts, elapsed decode interval and prefill interval. The reporter measures rank launch to first model text with one monotonic clock. Both rank receipts bind to the exact execution. Record the cold/warm label for each timed short case. No division by zero or fabricated rate. |
| `Rehearsal.visible_elapsed` | Elapsed time on the trusted WebKit harness’s monotonic clock from browser submission until the complete approved answer for that turn is visible. Require a successful publication and matching candidate, suite, case, repetition, job and attempt for both this observation and the native receipts. A missing render, denial, timeout or mismatched/replayed observation cannot count as a pass. |
| `Review.verdict` | One bounded structured verdict per case and blinded output digest. Admin-only authenticated entry for human scores. Keep trial-only text in private review storage, erase at review completion, cancellation or 24 hours, including after restart. |
| `Qualification.evidence` | Exact candidate and 005 basis, frozen suite, distinct job IDs covering each required run and injected fault, both receipts, correlated browser observations for every timed case, cleanup and reviewer verdicts. Bind to device/OS/runtime/policy identities. Exclude synthetic, stale or incomplete evidence. |

A candidate progresses from metadata review to probe approval, probe execution and cleanup, then to full-pack approval or a recorded refusal. Revalidate the stage authorization before each transfer. Verify exact downloaded files before admitting the full trial. Subsequent jobs progress through reserved, running and cleaning. Failed qualification retains the bounded reason. Only a complete report is eligible for an admin promotion. Drift or withdrawal fences current work. Revoking the comparable transport qualification also removes the basis for new large-model work. Restart/restore requires fresh measurements and reconciliation.
