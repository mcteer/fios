# Validation on fios-core

The following Make targets are implementation deliverables. Use the repository root on the Mini with the existing Spec Kit tooling and OrbStack preview.

1. Run `make check` and capture revision, preview health and current resources. Use private temporary state, unique test job labels and the existing disposable enrollment PKI. The two fake worker identities bind to separate synthetic Studio slots.
2. Run `make scheduling-test`. Save a submission envelope with its ledger incarnation. Submit it concurrently and after ordinary client and core restarts, then change its profile. Verify one job, one creation event and a conflict for the changed request. Fill the queue and test retry guidance and owner isolation.
3. In the same suite run 100 competing claim trials on independent connections. Exercise stale, missing and insufficient headroom, warm preference, one-slot contention and all-or-none paired grants. Drop a ready acknowledgment and confirm no participant starts until both are ready.
4. Run `make scheduling-acceptance`. Start the native core and two fake worker processes on loopback. Submit, poll, start, renew and complete through the real operator and worker interfaces. Inspect job history and usage, then repeat the completion. Confirm one settlement. With a synthetic tariff of USD 0.01 per measured token, finish with 10 measured input tokens and 4 estimated output tokens. Verify both labels, a measured subtotal of 10 tokens and USD 0.10 marked partial, and identical totals after replay. Changing only the output tag to measured on replay must conflict. Repeat with estimated-only and unknown-only usage and verify cost stays unknown.
5. Crash after granting, cancel while running, expire a lease, revoke a peer, lose one paired participant and race unload with a new claim. Assert rejection of late results and zero premature releases. Restart, reconcile both slots and prove a subsequent eligible job finishes. Take a snapshot while job A is queued. Complete A, then submit and complete job B after the snapshot. Save both original submission envelopes. Restore offline, restart, reconcile both workers and reopen admission. Retry both envelopes. Expect ledger_changed for each, zero new jobs, grants, executions or usage, A fenced and B absent. Verify that an explicitly fresh envelope succeeds. Restore the same snapshot again and verify the first restored incarnation is also rejected.
6. Interrupt the harness and repeat a normal teardown. Verify removal of run-owned jobs, databases, keys, containers and tags, plus successful reads of the original dashboard and Mini sample. Run `make check` and record test counts, source revision and remaining rollout gates in [tasks](tasks.md).
